## ⭐ **SUMMARIES OF ALL SEVEN STORIES**  

(Each summary is grounded in the actual tab content.)

---

### **1. The Carousel Between Us**  

**Prompt:** “It was not your ordinary…”  
**Genre:** Romantic dream surrealism  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/RomanceNovel/The%20Carousel%20Between%20Us.html)  

Mary, recovering from surgery, dreams of Steve — the bandmate she freezes around — in a surreal, symbolic, emotionally charged dreamscape. The car is a composite of every car from her life. Her brothers blindfold Steve and put him in the passenger seat. When she reaches across him to buckle his seatbelt, a shockwave erupts — a literal, physical jolt that freezes time and echoes through the dream. The dream pauses, resumes, and becomes a shared emotional event. The story blends reality, dream physics, symbolism, and romantic inevitability.  

**Core:** Destiny, surreal connection, dream logic, emotional electricity.

---

### **2. Nevermore Playing Games at Night**  

**Prompt:** “S/he used to think the worst thing that could happen was…”  
**Genre:** Psychological horror  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/Short_Stories/Nevermore%20Playing%20Games%20at%20Night.html)  

A gamer playing a Poe mystery game becomes trapped in a terrifying feedback loop where the game’s events mirror the real world. Scraping branches sync with in‑game sounds. Shadows move. “Nevermore” echoes from everywhere. The lights flicker. The storm outside syncs with the storm inside the game. Reality and game bleed together until the final reveal — it was the myna bird and the cat, but the terror was real enough to break him.  

**Core:** Atmosphere, dread, sensory horror, perfect pacing.

## **3. Blessed by the Cougar**  

**Prompt:** “It wasn’t until after that…”  
**Genre:** Mythic realism / spiritual awakening  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/Short_Stories/Blessed%20by%20the%20Cougar.html)  

A young girl searching for her totem animal finally receives her blessing from a cougar at the Living Desert State Park. The cougar approaches, presses its forehead to hers, and purrs — marking her, choosing her, awakening something ancient in her bones. The story blends cultural heritage, animal connection, spiritual symbolism, and childhood wonder.  

**Core:** Identity, heritage, animal spirituality, mythic resonance.

---

### **4. Bathing Bebe**  

**Prompt:** “Some things are not as easy as they seem…”  
**Genre:** Humor / slice‑of‑life  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/Short_Stories/Bathing%20Bebe.html)  

Mel attempts to bathe her enormous, tornado‑surviving, fence‑clearing, chaos‑loving dog Bebe. The story is comedic, warm, and full of physical humor. Bebe is a character — clever, mischievous, expressive — and the bath becomes a battle of wits involving peanut butter, hoses, mud, and dog logic.  

**Core:** Humor, chaos, animal personality, relatable domestic comedy.

---

### **5. The Truth in the Space Between**  

**Prompts:**  

- “…then the trunk lid slammed…”  
- “…what is held between the spaces…”  
**Genre:** Emotional realism / literary fiction  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/Short_Stories/The%20Truth%20in%20the%20Space%20Between.html)  

A mother sends her son Sean away to escape the shadow of his father. The story is built on “spaces” — the emotional pauses, the unsaid truths, the memories that live between moments. The trunk lid slamming becomes a symbol of endings and beginnings. The storm mirrors the emotional tension. The story moves through memory, grief, hope, and the quiet ache of letting go.  

**Core:** Emotional depth, symbolism, generational trauma, quiet truth.

---

### **6. The Fat Man with Too Small of a Coat**  

**Prompt:** “…but at the end of the day…”  
**Genre:** Heartfelt memoir / animal tribute  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/Short_Stories/The%20Fat%20Man%20with%20Too%20Small%20of%20a%20Coat.html)  

Chewy the cat arrives in the most chaotic way possible — spat out by a bulldog. He’s flea‑infested, slimy, terrified, and odd. The story follows his rescue, his quirks, his turkey obsession, his stuffed gator “wife,” his fear, his humor, and his life as a beloved family member. It’s funny, tender, and deeply emotional.  

**Core:** Love, rescue, animal personality, grief wrapped in humor.

---

## **7. The Strangest Thing Was the Quiet**  

**Prompt:** “…but the strangest thing was…”  
**Genre:** Intimate emotional surrealism  
**Tab:**   [leawildcat.github.io](https://leawildcat.github.io/Short_Stories/The%20Strangest%20Thing%20Was%20the%20Quiet.html)  

The narrator, recovering from knee surgery and grieving Joey, finds unexpected comfort in Trent’s presence. The story is built on micro‑intimacy — shared coffee, piano duets, synchronized cooking, quiet companionship. The surrealism is emotional, not dreamlike: the house feels “comfortable” instead of empty, the silence feels shared, and grief dissolves in the warmth of connection.  

**Core:** Healing, emotional surrealism, domestic intimacy, quiet transformation.
